About Gentle Dental Care

Gentle Dental Care is Georgia’s premier clinic for dental implants and general dentistry. With locations in Midtown Atlanta, the highly skilled dentists and prosthodontists at Gentle Dental Care are ready to give their patients a beautiful and healthy smile in a serene environment and at an affordable cost.

The adept and compassionate staff is excited to provide expert care in a wide range of medical and cosmetic services, from Invisalign® and laser teeth whitening to zirconia crowns and advanced periodontal care. And everything in the office is done on the most up-to-date equipment with the most state-of-the-art techniques, such as digital X-rays and an in-house lab for crowns and dentures.

Gentle Dental Care’s most notable specialty is dental implants, and their experienced team of dentists is ready to provide a variety of high-quality implants. The practice’s association with premier dental implant centers is a guarantee that the care provided is up to the highest standards and offers unparalleled results.

At Gentle Dental Care, rest assured that the patient’s needs come first. With a focus on patient comfort, an emphasis on preventive care, and a comprehensive offering of services, you’ll find yourself with a smile that’s never been brighter.
