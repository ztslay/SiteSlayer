Your child's smile is our priority!

Welcome to Children’s Dentistry of Atlanta, where we have had the honor of caring for children and adolescents since 2001. Our team wholeheartedly believes that visits to the dentist can be both enjoyable and educational, and we work every day to ensure that this belief is reflected in all we do.

We’ve established a safe and welcoming space where kids feel valued and supported. We take the time to develop healthy, positive relationships with all of our patients, so that we can be sure we’re providing the services they need in the manner that’s most appropriate for them. Perhaps most important of all, we truly enjoy getting to work with young people and their loved ones.

Through the decades, this unwavering focus on patient care has made our practice a trusted source of quality pediatric dentistry in Sandy Springs. We are proud that so many area families continue to entrust us with their children’s oral health, and we are committed to always remaining worthy of that trust.

Nitrous Oxide & Sedation Dentistry

Our office is a trusted source of comprehensive pediatric dental care, with services that include:

  • Pediatric Dental Exams & Cleanings: For young patients, a lifetime of bright smiles begins with regularly scheduled checkups and cleanings.
  • Preventive Pediatric Dental Care: In addition to exams and cleanings, our preventive services also include digital x-rays and imaging, fluoride treatment, and age-appropriate oral health education.
  • Pediatric Dental Fillings & Crowns: If a young patient develops a cavity or incurs other dental damage, we can address the problem with an array of restorative options.  
  • Safe Pediatric Dental Sedation: Safe sedation can be the ideal option for children who would otherwise find it difficult to remain calm and comfortable while receiving dental care.
  • Patients With Special Needs: Our office is a welcoming and inclusive place for all. We’re happy to work with the families of children with special needs to provide the accommodations they need to have a successful experience with us.

Financial Policy

Our office operates on a “fee for service” basis. Payment is expected at the time services are rendered. We accept cash, debit/credit cards, and checks.

We are not in-network with any commercial or state insurance plans. As a courtesy to our patients who have dental insurance, we offer electronic filing. Full payment is expected at the time of service; we will then submit dental claims on the patient’s behalf and request that the dental insurance company send reimbursement directly to the policy holder.
