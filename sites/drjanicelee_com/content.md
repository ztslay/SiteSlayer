Your child's smile is our priority!

Welcome to Children’s Dentistry of Atlanta, where we have had the honor of caring for children and adolescents since 2001. Our team wholeheartedly believes that visits to the dentist can be both enjoyable and educational, and we work every day to ensure that this belief is reflected in all we do.

We’ve established a safe and welcoming space where kids feel valued and supported. We take the time to develop healthy, positive relationships with all of our patients, so that we can be sure we’re providing the services they need in the manner that’s most appropriate for them. Perhaps most important of all, we truly enjoy getting to work with young people and their loved ones.

Through the decades, this unwavering focus on patient care has made our practice a trusted source of quality pediatric dentistry in Sandy Springs. We are proud that so many area families continue to entrust us with their children’s oral health, and we are committed to always remaining worthy of that trust.

## A Lifetime of Healthy Smiles Starts Here

Our team specializes in pediatric dental treatment, so we understand the unique needs involved in caring for the oral health of children, adolescents, and teenagers. We offer a range of services and provide gentle and compassionate care in an environment that’s comfortable for our patients and their families.

## Pediatric Dental Services We Offer

Pediatric Dental Exams & Cleanings:  Routine dental exams and cleanings are the key to a happy and healthy smile for your child. Our gentle but thorough cleanings will remove plaque and tartar and our comprehensive exams help us catch early warning signs of any problems so we can protect your child’s oral health.

Preventive Dental Care:  Our approach focuses on preventive care and early detection through services including fluoride treatments, dental sealants, and education on proper oral hygiene practices. We are dedicated to creating a foundation of dental health and habits that will last a lifetime.

Children’s Fillings, Crowns, and Restorative Care:  If your child needs a filling, crown, or any other type of restorative dental care, our team is here for you. We offer treatments and solutions to restore the functions and aesthetics of your child’s smile.

Dentistry for Patients with Special Needs:  Our team wants to ensure every patient has access to compassionate care centered around their individual needs. We’re experienced in providing dental services to patients with special needs.

Nitrous Oxide and Sedation Dentistry for Children:  For patients who may experience anxiety during dental appointments or who need to undergo longer or more complex procedures, our office can administer nitrous oxide and other safe, effective sedation dentistry options for children.

Pediatric Dental Emergencies:  A child’s dental emergencies can be stressful, but we’re here to help you. Our team is prepared to provide prompt and effective treatment for dental injuries and emergencies, offering peace of mind and immediate care when you need it most.

Cookies
