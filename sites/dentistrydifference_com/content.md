## Albert P. Nordone, DDS

As the owner of the practice, Dr. Nordone looks forward to welcoming you to Dentistry With A Difference. Dr. Nordone graduated from Emory University School of Dentistry where he received his D.D.S. degree in 1986. Dr. Nordone has been in practice in his current Dunwoody location since 1987.

[Dr. Nordone](https://dentistrydifference.com/about/dr-albert-nordone/) is a member of the American Dental Association, Georgia Dental Association, Northern District Dental Society, Hinman Dental Society, and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry. He served as Chairman of several committees in these organizations and sat on the Executive Council of the Northern District Dental Society for 3 years. His postgraduate education has included completion of all six continuums at the Pankey Institute in Florida. Dr. Nordone is a graduate of the world-renowned Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies. This institute provides rigorous postgraduate training in [advanced cosmetic techniques](https://dentistrydifference.com/dental-services/cosmetic-dentistry/).

Dr. Nordone is married with 4 children. He enjoys time with his family and participating in his children’s activities. He and his wife, Daphne, are very active in their church and vigorously support several charitable organizations.


## Hannah Oh, DDS

[Dr. Hannah Oh](https://dentistrydifference.com/about/dr-hannah-oh/) attended the University of Florida in Gainesville, FL for her undergraduate studies where she received her Bachelor’s Degree in Nutritional Sciences. She received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from UCLA School of Dentistry in 2007. After graduating from dental school, Dr. Oh furthered her dental education at the University of Florida in an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) Residency Program in Jacksonville, FL. This residency provided Dr. Oh with extensive training and experience in comprehensive multidisciplinary oral health care, full mouth reconstruction, surgical placement and restoration of [dental implants](https://dentistrydifference.com/dental-services/reconstructive-dentistry/dental-implants-dunwoody-ga/), endodontics, and esthetic dentistry.

Dr. Oh is passionate about building lifelong relationships and providing exceptional care to her patients. She believes that a commitment to continuing education is key and has completed hundreds of hours of continuing education in all disciplines of dentistry. She received training in sedation dentistry from the Dental Organization for Conscious Sedation (DOCS) which has allowed her to provide dental care for her apprehensive patients. She is also a certified Invisalign provider. Dr. Oh is a member of the American Dental Association, Georgia Dental Association, Northern District Dental Society, Hinman Dental Society, and the Academy of [General Dentistry.](https://dentistrydifference.com/dental-services/general-dentistry/)

Dr. Oh lives in Morningside Atlanta. She spends her time away from dentistry traveling to fun places where she can taste test the local fare, crafting at home, and spending quality time with her husband and their three young boys.

## What is General Dentistry?

General Dentistry refers to a wide range of primary dental care procedures intended to identify, assess, and manage various oral health issues. It aims to assist patients in maintaining healthy teeth and gums, avoiding illness, and, when necessary, providing treatments that restore appearance and function. [General dentists](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/24448-dentist), like Dr. Nordone and Dr. Oh, treat patients of all ages, giving families access to complete dental care in one handy location.

Dental health is a vital aspect of overall health. Studies in medicine have found connections between common oral infections and systemic illnesses, including diabetes and cardiovascular disease. At Dentistry With a Difference, we aim to help you keep your gums, teeth, and other oral tissues in good condition by providing quality General Dentistry in Dunwoody, GA.

## General Dentistry Services for Families

Dr. Nordone and Dr. Oh of Dunwoody, Georgia know that keeping your family healthy is an honor. Our dental team makes every effort to treat your family like they would their own families. This family-focused caring is what sets us apart and why your neighbors voted us the Best Dentists in Dunwoody.

Dentistry with a Difference offers a wide range of procedures and treatment options essential to preserving and promoting good oral health for your family. To prevent, identify, or treat issues as soon as possible, Dr. Nordone and Dr. Oh create individualized dental hygiene plans that include examinations, cleanings, teeth scaling (if necessary), X-rays, and other diagnostic tools.
