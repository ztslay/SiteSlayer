Pediatric Laser Frenectomy: Cutting-Edge Care for Little Smiles

At Schwartz Dentistry for Children, we offer pediatric laser frenectomy, a state-of-the-art procedure that effectively addresses tongue-tie and other oral conditions. Our advanced technology for infant tongue-tie laser surgery allows for the precise release of restricted lingual frenula, improving tongue movement and oral function.

FAQs

What are the benefits of using laser technology for frenectomy?
Laser frenectomy offers advantages such as minimal bleeding, reduced infection risk, faster healing, and enhanced precision.
Does the infant feel pain during a laser frenectomy?
Infants typically experience minimal discomfort during an infant laser frenectomy. Local anesthesia is administered for their comfort.
How long does the laser surgery procedure take?
The duration varies but typically lasts 15 to 30 minutes. We work efficiently to minimize disruption to your child’s routine.
What conditions can be treated with infant laser frenectomy?
Infant laser frenectomy primarily addresses tongue-tie, improving breastfeeding, speech, and oral health.
