An Innovative Approach to Dental Health

Dental Associates of Atlanta has been providing the highest level of dental care since 1961. Continuing this tradition of excellence, Dr. Lauren Bowersox and Dr. Steven Rafeedie offer predictable, clinically focused dentistry at our state-of-the-art Atlanta dental office.

As one of the top general dentists in the Atlanta area, Drs. Bowersox and Rafeedie believe that each person deserves to be informed and empowered to make decisions about their oral healthcare. From implants and complex extractions to crowns, and all cosmetic dental services, our doctors and staff offer exceptional care for patients all ages.

Experience for yourself our passion for dentistry at Dental Associates of Atlanta, where kindness and attentiveness are at the heart of everything we do.
